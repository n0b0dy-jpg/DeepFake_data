## DeepFake_data
This is a training dataset for Deepfake detection.

### I. Overview
This dataset is specifically constructed for the training of Deepfake detection programs. It contains videos generated by the latest DeepFake technology, which will subsequently undergo a series of compression processes.
### II. Data Composition
Video data: Faces of different individuals in various scenarios. Among them, the genuine videos are mainly sourced from the original videos on social media platforms such as Bilibili and YouTube.
### III. Data Scale
The entire dataset consists of 200 video files, each with a duration of 15 seconds, and they have not been annotated yet.
