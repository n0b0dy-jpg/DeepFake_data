# DeepFake Detection Dataset
This is a training dataset for Deepfake detection.
***
### I. Overview
This dataset is specifically constructed for the training of Deepfake detection programs. It contains videos generated by the latest DeepFake technology, such as Open - Sora, MultiModal - DeepFake, FakeFormer, StableDiffusion and FaceSwap, which will subsequently undergo a series of compression processes.
***

### 📚II. Data Composition
***Scale:***  It includes a large number of image and video samples, specifically 1200 videos of about 15 seconds, 500 of them are genuine while the rest are generated by different kinds of methods and have undergone compression in different degrees and ways. These abundant data provide sufficient materials for model training and help the model learn the complex feature differences between real and forged content.<br>
<br>
***Video data:***   Faces of different individuals in various scenarios. Among them, the genuine videos are mainly sourced from the original videos on social media platforms such as Bilibili and YouTube.
